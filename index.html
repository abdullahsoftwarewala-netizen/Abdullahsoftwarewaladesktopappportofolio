About

A builder who ships complete products, not just features.

I’m Abdullah SoftwareWala, a software developer based in Pakistan building software end to end — the database, the API, the real-time layer, the AI logic and the interface people actually touch. Most of my work sits where communication platforms meet applied artificial intelligence.

My main focus is Phoneopia, a communication platform combining messaging, calling and AI, built on a PHP and Node.js backend with WebSockets and WebRTC, a MySQL data layer and a Flutter mobile client. Alongside it I develop AI assistants and agent systems — GINBABA and Rehan Bot among them — exploring how LLMs can hold context, use tools and carry out real tasks.

I’m equally comfortable debugging a WebSocket handshake, designing a MySQL schema, wiring an LLM tool-calling loop or polishing an interface until it feels right. I prefer shipping small, working systems and iterating over planning something perfect that never launches.
